# Demographic source extracts

Retrieved 26–27 October 2026. Run `node Atlas/source-data/build-demographics.cjs` from the repository root to regenerate `Atlas/demographics.js`, then `node Atlas/source-data/verify-demographics.cjs`.

- `population.json`: [World Bank SP.POP.TOTL, 2024](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/all/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL?format=json&date=2024&per_page=400), including source metadata. Country `id` is matched to the profile ISO alpha-2 code. World Bank data are available under [CC BY 4.0](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses#cc-by).
- Vatican City is absent from the World Bank series. The builder explicitly adds [882 residents as of 31 December 2024](https://www.vaticanstate.va/en/state-and-government/general-informations/population.html), including citizens and non-citizens.
- `religious-shares.json`: rounded 2020 percentage estimates extracted from the data embedded in Pew's [Religious Composition by Country, 2010–2020](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/feature/religious-composition-by-country-2010-2020/) interactive. The source includes broad affiliation groups and places outside Atlas's country scope. Small countries absent from this source remain missing. Use the rounded percentages, not the unrounded population counts.
- `state-religion.txt`: text extracted from [Pew's Appendix A](https://assets.pewresearch.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/11/2017/09/29162845/Appendix-A.pdf), published in 2017 and classifying government relationships with religion **as of 2015**. It is a historical snapshot, not a current constitutional inventory. Full-source counts are 43 official, 40 favored, 106 neither, and 10 hostile; Atlas excludes Western Sahara and five territories outside its profile set, and the source does not cover Vatican City. The resulting 194 matched classifications are 42, 40, 102, and 10 respectively.

Country-name aliases are explicit in the builder, including Turkey/Türkiye, Burma/Myanmar, Swaziland/Eswatini, and Palestinian territories/Palestine. Missing values are not inferred from nearby countries, religion, or membership in any organization.

The majority label requires a religious group above 50% of the rounded 2020 estimate. Unaffiliated is an affiliation category, not a religion; it includes people with varied personal beliefs. A largest group at or below 50% is a plurality, not a majority. Percentages displayed as zero in the rounded source are conservatively shown as <0.1%; this does not establish a nonzero presence. Broad categories do not establish denominations or observance. State classification is never inferred from population affiliation, and favored status is never displayed as official state religion.
