    A note on supported documents

    Designed for ordinary PDFs. Password-protected files and interactive forms aren’t supported. Digital signatures cannot remain valid after editing. Document-level bookmarks, attachments, and accessibility tags may not carry over.

    Images become A4 pages, fitted without cropping, with a white background and a 10 mm margin. Image orientation follows the decoded file. Exports preserve PDF page content; they do not compress or rasterize whole PDFs.
