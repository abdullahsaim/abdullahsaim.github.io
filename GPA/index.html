04 / THE OFFICIAL RULES

UOG grading reference

Compare revised undergraduate rules with the earlier grading table.

Which policy should I choose? UOG lists Revised Semester System Rules (2025) for undergraduate programs, effective from Fall 2025, alongside older semester rules. Choose the policy stated on your own official grade card. Every semester can have its own grading policy, so you can combine old and new transcripts without recalculating earlier semesters. This independent calculator does not cover special graduate/professional grading or repeat-course adjustments.
